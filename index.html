Who We Are

Building practical solutions for a changing world.

NCE SkyVolt Dynamics Ltd is a Nigerian engineering, energy and technology company providing practical solutions across solar energy, electrical systems, power solutions, security technology and related technical services.

Founded in 2026, we are developing a growing technical business focused on reliability, innovation and long-term partnerships.

Our operations are connected to Lagos and Enugu, Nigeria, with a long-term ambition to serve customers and partners across West Africa and the wider global market.
