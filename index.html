Who We Are

About NCE SkyVolt

A growing Nigerian company built around engineering, energy and technology.

Engineering with purpose.

NCE SkyVolt Dynamics Ltd was established to provide practical energy and engineering solutions within reach of customers in Nigeria, West Africa and eventually the wider global market.

Our activities span solar energy, electrical systems, power solutions, security technology and related technical services.

As a growing company, we are continuously developing our capabilities while building relationships with customers, manufacturers and strategic partners.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized engineering, energy and technology company delivering innovative, reliable and accessible solutions that improve how people live, work and operate.


Our Mission

To provide practical and dependable engineering, energy and technology solutions through technical expertise, innovation, responsible partnerships and customer-focused service.
